3. ADOPTION COHORTS
==============================================================================
TWFE with staggered timing is most misleading when effects differ
across cohorts. Early adopters also spend more of the sample
treated, so they carry more weight in the pooled estimate.

  early cohort (1991-1998): 9 states  AZ,CT,GA,IL,MA,NC,NJ,PA,WA
  late  cohort (2000-2012): 11 states  FL,LA,MD,MI,MO,NE,NM,NY,OH,SC,TX

  window k in [-5,+4]:
      all adopters                         N= 5855 beta=+0.3005 p=0.019 CI [+0.0421,+0.5436] *
      early only                           N= 4111 beta=+0.1946 p=0.333 CI [-0.2533,+0.5755]
      late only                            N= 4655 beta=+0.3525 p=0.049 CI [+0.0167,+0.7057] *

  window k in [-3,+2]:

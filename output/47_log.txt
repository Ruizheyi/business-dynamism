4. HOW TO READ THIS
==============================================================================
  leads flat, Wald does not reject -> the aggregate survives the
     check that killed the equipment gradient, and is the paper's
     one estimate that has passed a dynamic specification.
  leads trending, Wald rejects -> the aggregate is also a pre-trend
     and the paper has no causal estimate at all. That is a
     legitimate result but it changes what the paper says.
